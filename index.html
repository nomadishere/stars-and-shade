How the deck works

  1. The title is the who or what: a sign, a planet, a house, or one of the extras.
  2. The zinger is the vibe. It's the shade. It's how that energy would roast itself if it were being honest.
  3. The “I” motto is the job, the one thing that energy does. Memorize the mottos and you can read a chart.

Pro move: say the zinger, then the motto, out loud. “Not dramatic, just iconic. I will.” It sticks way faster when it's a little unhinged.
