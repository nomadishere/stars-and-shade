How the deck works

  1. The title is the who or what: a sign, a planet, a house, or one of the extras.
  2. The zinger is the vibe. It's the shade. It's how that energy would roast itself if it were being honest.
  3. The “I” motto is the job, the one thing that energy does. Memorize the mottos and you can read a chart.

Pro move: say the zinger, then the motto, out loud. “Not dramatic, just iconic. I will.” It sticks way faster when it's a little unhinged.

Astrology 101 (no cringe)

Your birth chart is a snapshot of the sky at the exact minute you were born. It doesn't control you. Think of it as a mirror with really good lighting.

The formula that makes it all click

Planet = WHATAn energy, a part of you. The Planet mottos are the verbs: I express, I attract, I act.
Sign = HOWThe style it moves in. The Sign mottos are the flavor: I desire, I build, I rebel.
House = WHEREThe area of life it plays out in. The House mottos are the arena: I partner, I value, I achieve.

Put them together and the mottos read like a sentence. Look:

Venus in Scorpio in the Seventh House
I attract + I desire + I partner
Loves intensely and wants all-or-nothing partnerships. Situationships? Not invited.
Mercury in Gemini in the Third House
I communicate + I think + I connect
Talks fast, texts faster, and genuinely knows a guy for everything.
Mars in Capricorn in the Tenth House
I act + I build + I achieve
Locked in on career goals and plays the long game. The five-year plan has a five-year plan.

Your Big Three

  • Sun (I express): who you are at the core. This is your “sign.”
  • Moon (I react): what you need to feel safe. Your emotional autopilot.
  • Rising / Ascendant (I approach): your first impression and how you walk into the room. Needs your exact birth time.

Elements and modes

  • Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) says go. Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) says build. Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) says talk it out. Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) says feel it first.
  • Cardinal signs start things (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn). Fixed signs keep things going (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius). Mutable signs adapt (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces).

The extras

  • Midheaven: your career north star, at the very top of the chart.
  • North Node and South Node: your growth edge and your comfort zone. Lean north, visit south.
  • Lot of Fortune: where luck and flow come easiest.
  • Retrograde: a planet's review season. Rethink, redo, reconnect. Not a reason to cancel your life.

How to actually use this

  1. Pull your chart. Make it right here: open the Nebula, tap My Sky, and enter your birth date, time and city.
  2. Start with your Big Three. Read each one with the formula and the mottos. That's already more than most people know.
  3. One card a day. The deck is a 44-day cycle. Watch the card, say the motto out loud, then notice where that energy shows up in your day.
  4. Journal it. Each card's “Use it” line in the card guide is a ready-made journal prompt.
  5. Keep an eye on the sky. When Mercury goes retrograde, review instead of launch. When Jupiter's in your corner, go bigger.
  6. Use it for self-awareness, not judgment. “He's a Gemini” is not a personality critique, bestie. It's a starting point for understanding.

Memory hack

The 12 Sign mottos in order read like a life story: I am, I have, I think, I feel, I will, I analyze, I balance, I desire, I see, I build, I rebel, I believe. Learn them as one chant and you'll never forget the order of the zodiac again.

Astrology is a vibe check, not a verdict. Use it to understand yourself and your people, never to box anyone in.
